Maqaam-e-Faiz / مقامِ فیض

Faiz Ahmed Faiz / فیض احمد فیض

Faiz Ahmed Faiz (13 February 1911 – 20 November 1984) was an Urdu and Punjabi progressive writer and poet. This project is an effort to publish all the works by Faiz. The inspiration for this project comes from Dr. Fran Pritchett's work on Ghalib and Meer.
